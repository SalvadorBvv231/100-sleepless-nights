# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-02

First release.

- Difficulty clock based on the world day, with `/sleepless status` and `/sleepless setday`.
- Five difficulty tiers with smoothly ramping mob effects (spawns, health, damage, door breaking, gear, creeper explosions, armored enchanted mobs).
- Friday Night Party: weekly horde that grows over time, with miners, builders, champions, XP and loot rewards, plus `/sleepless party start|stop`.
- Village changes: more and stronger iron golems, extra golem from day 50, villagers go indoors earlier from day 100.
- No diamond gear before day 150 (configurable).
- Full configuration in `config/sleepless100.cfg`.
